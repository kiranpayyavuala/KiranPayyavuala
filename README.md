### 🚀 Experience That Shapes My Approach

**Understand First. Break It Down. Automate Better.**

🔍 **Learn the manual process deeply** — follow it step by step, understand every action, and know why each step is required.

🧩 **Break complexity into smaller chunks** — divide complex problems into manageable pieces and implement them incrementally.

 ✅ **Validate before automating** — make sure the process is correct, reliable, and repeatable before turning it into automation.

⚙️ **Automate with understanding** — eliminate repetitive work and transform complex processes into simple, reliable automation.

 📈 **Measure and improve** — continuously look for ways to make processes faster, safer, more reliable, and easier to maintain.
